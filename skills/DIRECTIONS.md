# TAWD Design Directions (dropdown catalog, v3)

The PM picks **3 different directions** per client, one for each mockup. TAWD proposes 3 **after the inputs** (v3, 27 September 2026): from the client's brand (the logo's colours), real photos and studied reference sites, as well as the industry and mood words. The dropdown is the PM's override, shown after the proposal. Each direction is given to a different design skill. The "best skill" column says which one handles it most naturally.

If the client supplies full brand guidelines, their colors and fonts are locked in every direction. The direction then changes layout, structure, imagery and the signature element only.

## Selection rules

1. The 3 directions must be different, and each runs on a different skill.
2. **Emergency-mode trades** (plumbing, HVAC repair, garage doors, locksmith): at least one of the three must be Bold Emergency.
3. **Design log check:** before building, TAWD compares the chosen directions against the design log. A direction plus palette family already used for a competitor in the same city is flagged, and the PM picks another.
4. Heritage Established is only offered when the intake form contains a verifiable founding year.
5. **Gallery & Portfolio** is pre-selected only for creative businesses (rule in the table below). It needs the client's own project photos: every grid tile without a real photo is a visible `[PLACEHOLDER]`, never a stock or AI image passed off as their work, and each project page names only what the intake gives (project, place, year). The phone number and a clear enquiry button stay visible on every page, as for every direction.
6. Every direction still follows `TAWD-PROFILE.md`: the conversion floor, house rules, desktop mockup, and the small VML credit in the footer.

## How TAWD pre-selects

| Intake signal | Suggested directions |
|---|---|
| Emergency trade | Bold Emergency + Modern Clean + Warm Local |
| Legal or financial | Classic Trust + Editorial Authority + Premium Upscale |
| Med spa, dental or wellness | Clinical Calm + Premium Upscale + Modern Clean |
| Heavy trades (drilling, roofing, excavation) | Rugged Craftsman + Classic Trust + Modern Clean |
| Pest control | Classic Trust + Modern Clean + Editorial Authority |
| Trade entered as "other" | Read from the business name and services first (pest, termite, plumbing, HVAC, roofing and so on), then the matching row |
| Mood words "friendly", "family", "local" | Swap one pick for Warm Local or Bright & Friendly |
| Mood words "premium", "luxury", "high-end" | Swap one pick for Premium Upscale |
| Full brand guide supplied | Brand-Led + the two closest matches |
| Creative trade (Interior design, Art & decorative finishes, Photography, Architecture and remodeling), or any business whose name, services or mood words mention interior design, art, photography, remodeling, renovation, architecture, a portfolio or a gallery | Gallery & Portfolio + Premium Upscale + Classic Trust |

## Proposed after the inputs (v3)

Applied on top of the table above, in this order. Inputs beat rules.

| Input | Effect |
|---|---|
| Brand colours deep and sober (the main colour dark) | Classic Trust leads |
| Brand colours bright (two or more saturated mid-tones) | Bright & Friendly leads |
| Brand colours dark with gold | Premium Upscale is one of the picks |
| No real photos yet | Photo-led directions (Premium Upscale, Warm Local, Gallery & Portfolio) move down and the type-led Editorial Authority takes their place; the note says results will be weaker |
| A team photo, and no people-first pick yet | Warm Local is one of the picks |
| Reference sites studied | The direction 2 or more of them are closest to leads; a direction one points to is one of the picks |

Colours taken from the logo are the client's brand but not a brand guide: they don't make Brand-Led lead. Every concept still uses them.
