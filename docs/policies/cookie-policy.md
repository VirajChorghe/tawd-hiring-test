## What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your device. They help the site work, remember your choices, and, with your permission, help us understand how visitors use the site.

**Strictly necessary.** These keep the site working and secure, and remember your cookie choices. They are always on.

[[END]]

[[END]]

## Your choices

[[IF tracking.consent_mode == "ask_first"]]
Analytics and advertising cookies are only set after you accept them in the cookie banner. You can change your choice at any time using the **Cookie settings** link at the bottom of every page.
[[END]]
[[IF tracking.consent_mode == "opt_out"]]
You can turn off analytics and advertising cookies at any time using the **Cookie settings** or **"Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information"** link at the bottom of every page. This site also honors the Global Privacy Control (GPC) browser signal.
[[END]]

You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may stop parts of the site from working.
