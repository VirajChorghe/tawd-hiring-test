### Nevada residents: consumer health data

This part is our consumer health data privacy policy under Nevada law.

- **What we collect and why.** If you tell us about your health through our forms, by email or by phone (for example a treatment you're interested in, a condition or a medication), we collect it only to answer you and provide the services you ask for.
- **Where it comes from.** From you.
- **Who we share it with.** Only the service providers that run this site and our messages, such as {{site.hosting_provider}}, to do that work for us. We do not sell consumer health data, and we do not share it for any other purpose without your consent.
- **Your rights.** You can ask us to confirm whether we collect, share or sell your consumer health data; to give you a list of every third party we have shared it with, and how to contact them; to stop collecting, sharing or selling it; and to delete it.
- **How to ask.** Email {{business.privacy_email}} or call {{business.phone}}. We respond within 45 days, and may extend by 45 more days if we tell you why. If we decline your request, you can appeal by emailing {{business.privacy_email}} with "Privacy appeal" in the subject.
- **No geofencing.** We do not use a virtual boundary around any health care facility to track or message you.
