## Utah residents

If you live in Utah, the Utah Consumer Privacy Act gives you the right to confirm whether we process your personal data and to access it; to correct inaccuracies; to delete personal data you gave us; to get a portable copy of data you gave us; and to opt out of targeted advertising and the sale of personal data. To make a request, email {{business.privacy_email}} or call {{business.phone}}. We respond within 45 days.
