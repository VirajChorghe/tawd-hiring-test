## New Jersey residents

If you live in New Jersey, the New Jersey Data Privacy Act gives you the right to confirm whether we process your personal data and to access it; to correct it; to delete it; to get a portable copy; and to opt out of targeted advertising, the sale of personal data, and certain profiling. We treat a Global Privacy Control signal as a request to opt out. To make a request, email {{business.privacy_email}} or call {{business.phone}}. We respond within 45 days, and may extend once by 45 more days if we tell you why. If we decline your request, you can appeal by emailing {{business.privacy_email}} with "Privacy appeal" in the subject. We will reply in writing with our reasons; if you are not satisfied, you can contact the New Jersey Division of Consumer Affairs. If we materially change how we use your data, we will update this policy and its effective date.
