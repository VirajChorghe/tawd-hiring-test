## Louisiana residents

From January 1, 2027, if you live in Louisiana, the Louisiana Data Privacy Act gives you the right to confirm whether we process your personal data and to access it; to correct it; to delete it; and to opt out of targeted advertising, the sale of personal data, and certain profiling. We treat a Global Privacy Control signal as a request to opt out. To make a request, email {{business.privacy_email}} or call {{business.phone}}. We will verify your identity before acting on it.
