## Nebraska residents

If you live in Nebraska, the Nebraska Data Privacy Act gives you the right to confirm whether we process your personal data and to access it; to correct it; to delete it; to get a portable copy; and to opt out of targeted advertising, the sale of personal data, and certain profiling. We will not sell sensitive personal data without your consent. To make a request, email {{business.privacy_email}} or call {{business.phone}}. We may need to verify your identity. We respond within 45 days, and may extend once by 45 more days if we tell you why. If we decline your request, you can appeal by emailing {{business.privacy_email}} with "Privacy appeal" in the subject. We will reply within 60 days, and if we deny your appeal you can file a complaint with the Nebraska Attorney General.
