## Connecticut residents

If you live in Connecticut, the Connecticut Data Privacy Act gives you the right to confirm whether we process your personal data and to access it; to correct it; to delete it; to get a portable copy; and to opt out of targeted advertising, the sale of personal data, and certain profiling. We do not collect, use or sell personal data to train large language models. We treat a Global Privacy Control signal as a request to opt out of targeted advertising and sale. To make a request, email {{business.privacy_email}} or call {{business.phone}}. If we decline your request, you can appeal by emailing {{business.privacy_email}} with "Privacy appeal" in the subject. We will reply in writing within 60 days, and if we deny your appeal we will tell you how to contact the Connecticut Attorney General.
