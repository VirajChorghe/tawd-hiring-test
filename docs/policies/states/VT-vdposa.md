## Vermont residents

If you live in Vermont, once the Vermont Data Privacy and Online Surveillance Act takes effect it gives you the right to confirm whether we process your personal data and to access it; to correct it; to delete it; to get a portable copy; to opt out of targeted advertising, the sale of personal data, and certain profiling; and to get a list of third parties your data was sold to. We do not collect, use or sell personal data to train large language models. We treat a Global Privacy Control signal as a request to opt out. To make a request, email {{business.privacy_email}} or call {{business.phone}}. If we decline your request, you can appeal by emailing {{business.privacy_email}} with "Privacy appeal" in the subject. We will reply within 60 days, and if we deny your appeal you can contact the Vermont Attorney General.
