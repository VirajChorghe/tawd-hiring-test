### Your California privacy rights

If you live in California, you can ask {{business.legal_name}} to:

- tell you what personal information we collected about you, where it came from, why we collected it and who we disclosed it to;
- delete or correct it;
- stop selling or sharing it for cross-context behavioral advertising (we also honor Global Privacy Control).

We do not sell personal information for money. The personal information we collect is your contact details and your message (from you), and device and browsing information (from the site[[IF tracking.any]] and, only with your consent, from cookies and tags[[END]])[[IF tracking.call_rail]], and the details of calls to our call tracking numbers (from our call tracking provider)[[END]]. We use it to answer you and provide our services[[IF tracking.any]], and with your consent to measure visits and our advertising[[END]]. We will not treat you differently for using these rights.

To make a request, email {{business.privacy_email}} or call {{business.phone}}. We may need to verify your identity. An authorized agent may make a request for you.
