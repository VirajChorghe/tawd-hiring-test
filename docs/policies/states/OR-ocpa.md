## Oregon residents

If you live in Oregon, the Oregon Consumer Privacy Act gives you the right to confirm whether we process your personal data and to access it; to correct it; to delete it; to get a portable copy; to opt out of targeted advertising, the sale of personal data, and certain profiling; and to ask for a list of the specific third parties (other than individuals) we have disclosed your personal data to. We treat a Global Privacy Control signal as a request to opt out. To make a request, email {{business.privacy_email}} or call {{business.phone}}. We respond within 45 days, and may extend once by 45 more days if we tell you why. If we decline your request, you can appeal by emailing {{business.privacy_email}} with "Privacy appeal" in the subject. If we deny your appeal, you can contact the Oregon Department of Justice.
