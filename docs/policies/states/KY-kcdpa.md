## Kentucky residents

If you live in Kentucky, the Kentucky Consumer Data Protection Act gives you the right to confirm whether we process your personal data and to access it; to correct it; to delete it; to get a portable copy; and to opt out of targeted advertising, the sale of personal data, and certain profiling. To make a request, email {{business.privacy_email}} or call {{business.phone}}. You don't need an account. We respond within 45 days, and may extend once by 45 more days if we tell you why. Requests are free up to twice a year. If we decline your request, you can appeal by emailing {{business.privacy_email}} with "Privacy appeal" in the subject. We will reply in writing within 60 days, and if we deny your appeal you can file a complaint with the Kentucky Attorney General.
