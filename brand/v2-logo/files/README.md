# TAWD logo (approved by Kevin): Option 3, "both Vs"

Files:
- tawd-logo.svg: full lockup on light (icon + wordmark + "BY VELOCITY")
- tawd-logo-dark.svg: full lockup on dark navy
- tawd-icon.svg / tawd-icon-dark.svg: icon only
- tawd-favicon.svg: tiny sizes (16-32px): the V only

The idea:
- Icon: the agent's face (glasses) with a two-tone V "smile" (blue left arm, cyan right arm) in place of the two bars.
- Wordmark: the "w" is drawn as two V's; the first V is blue (cyan on dark). The rest of the wordmark is navy (white on dark). The "a" is no longer blue.
- Caption: "BY VELOCITY", small, uppercase, letter-spaced, right-aligned under the wordmark.

Colors: navy #0E1A3A · blue #2B59FF · cyan #22C7E0 · caption #4A5677 (light) / #C9D2EA (dark)
Font: Plus Jakarta Sans 800 (wordmark), 600 (caption). The SVGs use live text; convert the text to outlines (or use the app's font) for production, and fine-tune the letter spacing around the drawn "w".
